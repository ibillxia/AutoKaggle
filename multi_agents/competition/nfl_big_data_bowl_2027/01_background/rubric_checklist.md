# Rubric checklist

## Football (30%)
- [ ] Would NFL teams or the league office use this on a week-to-week basis?
- [ ] Does the analysis account for the variables that make football data complex (draft capital, opportunity, position, exposure, scheme)?
- [ ] Are the ideas unique relative to conventional scouting wisdom?

## Data Science (30%)
- [ ] Is the work correct and reproducible?
- [ ] Are the claims backed by data with stated uncertainty?
- [ ] Are the statistical models appropriate for the sample size and structure?
- [ ] Are the analytical applications innovative?

## Writeup (20%)
- [ ] Is the writeup well written and easy to follow?
- [ ] Is the motivation (metric, player evaluation goal) clearly defined?

## Visualization (20%)
- [ ] Are the charts and tables accessible to a football audience?
- [ ] Are they accurate (no truncated axes, no misleading aggregation)?
- [ ] Are they innovative?

## Pass / fail gates
- [ ] Explicitly links Combine sensor tracking data to regular-season NFL game performance.
- [ ] Readable markdown writeup with embedded visuals and an attached public Kaggle Notebook.

## Hard limits
- max_words: 2000
- max_tables_and_figures: 9
- notebook_required: True
- must_use_tracking_data: True
