=== [11] ? (2026-10-18 06:29:45.137000) votes=6 ===
Some data on this question: we tried adding the public 18-model corpus (licensed rows only, ~15k texts) to a simple char+word TF-IDF + LinearSVC, and scored with GroupKFold by prompt on competition texts only.

- competition data only: 0.326
- + 850 external rows that are already single paragraphs: 0.308
- + 14,911 external rows flattened to one paragraph: 0.279

We saw the same direction with a stronger TF-IDF and with frozen Qwen3-8B embeddings. Our main suspect is the mismatch: competition texts have 0% line breaks and 0% bold, while 94% / 60% of the external answers do, and the external median length is 174 words vs 101. So "more data" may matter, but only if it looks like the host's data.

Details and code: https://www.kaggle.com/code/mizuno0522/model-fingerprints-punctuation-external-data
