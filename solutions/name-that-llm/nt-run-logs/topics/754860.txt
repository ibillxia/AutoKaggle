=== [4] ? (2026-11-04 01:07:28.270000) votes= ===
Best single model here: **0.628 public**, a Qwen3-8B LoRA, nothing else in the submission.

- **Data:** competition train plus the community Open Pool (v10), capped per label. The same recipe trained on our older, smaller pool reached 0.5886 OOF; with the Open Pool it reached 0.6224 OOF (5 folds: 0.589 / 0.598 / 0.654 / 0.626 / 0.645). Like the others in this thread, nearly all of the gain came from the data, not from the model.
- **Training:** a Kaggle TPU v5e-8 session in bfloat16. Getting the TPU took a long queue, but the run itself was short.
- **Inference:** fp16 on a T4 with a bfloat16 retry for any batch that overflows. The public score (0.628) came out a little above the OOF (0.6224), as it has for our other LoRA members.
- **In a blend:** the same model as the main member, plus an older LoRA at a small weight, a DeBERTa, three n-gram SVCs and a small stylometry model, scores 0.635. So the ensemble adds less than a point on top of a good single model.

The OOF here is plain balanced accuracy on held-out prompts; per-fold spread is large (0.589 to 0.654), so I would not read much into single-model gaps under ~0.01 between people's numbers.
